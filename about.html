About Us

The people and principles behind the Mateo University Institutional Exam Portal.

Our story

Mateo University created the Institutional Exam Portal to bring examinations into one secure, easy-to-use system. Students, faculty and administrators share a single place for taking exams, reviewing results and managing courses.

Our mission

To make assessment simple, fair and secure, so teachers can focus on teaching and students on showing what they know.
