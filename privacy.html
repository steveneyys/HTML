Privacy Notice

How the Mateo University Exam Portal collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This notice follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and also explains rights under the GDPR and the CCPA/CPRA for people those laws cover.

Who we are

Mateo University runs the Institutional Exam Portal and decides how personal data in it is used. Contact details are at the end of this notice.

What we collect

  • Account details: full name, username, role, section and assigned subjects.
  • Password: stored only as a salted one-way hash. We cannot read it.
  • Exam activity: answers, scores, results, attempt dates and times, flagged questions and retake permissions.
  • Session data: a session cookie and browser storage that keep you signed in and save exam progress.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as health, biometric or financial information.

How we use it

  • To create your account and confirm who you are.
  • To deliver exams, score them and show results.
  • To let faculty and administrators manage courses, sections, attempts and grades.
  • To keep the portal secure and meet academic record-keeping and legal duties.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • You see your own account, exams and results.
  • Faculty see students, attempts and answers for the subjects they teach.
  • Administrators see accounts, subjects, sections and overall statistics.
  • Our database provider processes data on our behalf to host it.
  • Authorities may receive data when the law requires it.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase, which may be outside the Philippines. We keep it while your account is active and for as long as Mateo University's academic records policy requires, then delete or anonymize it. Passwords are hashed, access depends on your role, and session cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite (and Secure over HTTPS). No online system is perfectly secure, and we will notify you and the authorities of a breach as the law requires.

Cookies

We use one session cookie to keep you signed in, plus browser storage for your session and exam progress. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Philippines: you may be informed, object, access, correct, erase or block your data, move your data, claim damages, and complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

GDPR (if it applies): you may access, correct, delete or restrict your data, receive a copy, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you may ask what we collect and use, and request deletion or correction. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Email mateouniversity@mu.edu.ph to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and some academic records must be kept.

Minors and changes

The portal is for university students and staff. If a student is under the age of majority, a parent or guardian may contact us about their data. We may update this notice, and the date above shows the latest change.
