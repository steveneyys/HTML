Examination Guidelines & FAQ

  • Do not switch tabs or minimize the browser during an exam.
  • You will be asked to confirm before starting. If an access code is required, the timer starts after the correct code is entered.
  • The timer keeps running even if you refresh or close the page. Reopen the exam from your dashboard to resume.
  • Answers are saved as you go.
  • Some exams randomly select questions from a larger question bank. Students may receive different sets.
  • Your selected questions and their order stay the same when you refresh or resume an ongoing attempt.
  • Some exams need an access code from your instructor.
  • Each exam has a limited number of attempts. Ask your instructor if you need a retake.
  • Where your instructor allows it, you can review your answers after submitting.
